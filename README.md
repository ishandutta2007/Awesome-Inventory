## Top Inventory Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Stock Control, Multi-Warehouse, Order Fulfillment, Manufacturing Inventory & Multi-Channel Inventory*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Inventory Management**. These systems track stock levels, manage warehouses, handle purchasing and sales orders, support barcodes/serials/batches, and often integrate with ecommerce, accounting, and manufacturing.



**Examples** include Cin7, Katana, Zoho Inventory, Fishbowl, Unleashed, DEAR Systems (Cin7 Core), SkuVault, Sortly, inFlow Inventory, and Finale Inventory (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Inventory management has strong open-source options. **InvenTree**, **ERPNext**, and **Odoo Community** provide production-capable stock control, multi-warehouse support, and extensibility. Additional tools cover specialized and lightweight use cases. This section is heavily expanded.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Cin7](https://www.cin7.com/)**  

  Cloud inventory and order management platform strong in multi-channel selling, EDI, 3PL automation, and warehouse operations for product sellers.



- **[Katana](https://katanamrp.com/)**  

  Cloud manufacturing and inventory platform focused on real-time production planning, material requirements, and live stock visibility for makers.



- **[Zoho Inventory](https://www.zoho.com/inventory/)**  

  Affordable multi-channel inventory software tightly integrated with the Zoho ecosystem—orders, warehouses, shipping, and basic manufacturing support.



- **[Fishbowl](https://www.fishbowlinventory.com/)**  

  Inventory and warehouse management system popular with manufacturers and distributors, known for QuickBooks integration and modular warehouse features.



- **[Unleashed](https://www.unleashedsoftware.com/)**  

  Cloud inventory platform for product businesses covering purchasing, sales, production, multi-warehouse stock, and forecasting.



- **[DEAR Systems / Cin7 Core](https://www.cin7.com/)**  

  Inventory, manufacturing, and order management solution (now part of the Cin7 family) aimed at growing product and manufacturing businesses.



- **[SkuVault](https://www.skuvault.com/)**  

  Warehouse and inventory management platform focused on ecommerce and multi-channel sellers with barcode-driven warehouse workflows.



- **[Sortly](https://www.sortly.com/)**  

  Visual, easy-to-use inventory tracking software popular with small businesses, field teams, and asset-style inventory needs.



- **[inFlow Inventory](https://www.inflowinventory.com/)**  

  Inventory management software for small to mid-sized businesses covering purchasing, sales, stock control, and basic manufacturing.



- **[Finale Inventory](https://www.finaleinventory.com/)**  

  Cloud inventory system for multi-channel sellers with strong ecommerce integrations, warehouse tools, and order fulfillment support.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[InvenTree](https://github.com/inventree/InvenTree)**  

  Dedicated open-source inventory management system with powerful stock control, part tracking, BOMs, plugins, REST API, and low-level inventory operations (MIT license).



- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Full open-source ERP with robust inventory, multi-warehouse, serial/batch tracking, purchasing, sales, and manufacturing modules—widely used by SMEs (GPL-3.0).



- **[Odoo Community (Inventory / Stock)](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Modular open-source ERP including a mature Inventory app for warehouses, routes, barcodes, lots/serials, and multi-company stock management (LGPL for Community).



- **[PartKeepr](https://github.com/partkeepr/PartKeepr)**  

  Open-source inventory management originally designed for electronic components, with part databases, stock locations, and project tracking.



- **[MyCompany (lsFusion)](https://github.com/lsfusion-solutions/mycompany)**  

  Free, self-hosted ERP/CRM for small businesses covering inventory, purchasing, sales, manufacturing, invoicing, and POS in one database.



- **[OpenBoxes](https://github.com/openboxes/openboxes)**  

  Open-source supply chain and inventory management system originally focused on health commodities and humanitarian logistics.



- **[Stock management modules in Tryton / other ERPs](https://github.com/)**  

  Open ERP frameworks with inventory and warehouse modules suitable for customization.



- **[Barcode and warehouse open utilities](https://github.com/)**  

  Libraries and small apps for barcode generation, scanning workflows, and simple stock movement tracking.



- **[Spreadsheet-to-inventory open converters and importers](https://github.com/)**  

  Tools that help migrate and maintain inventory data between open systems and common file formats.



- **[Plugin ecosystems for InvenTree & ERPNext](https://github.com/)**  

  Community plugins extending labeling, integrations, reporting, and ecommerce connections for open inventory platforms.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Deploying **InvenTree** when you need focused, API-first inventory and part tracking without a full ERP.

- Choosing **ERPNext** or **Odoo Community** when inventory must sit alongside accounting, CRM, purchasing, and manufacturing.

- Using **OpenBoxes** for logistics-heavy or multi-site distribution scenarios.

- Combining open inventory cores with commercial ecommerce or 3PL platforms via APIs.

- Accepting that deep multi-channel EDI, advanced 3PL automation, and polished native mobile warehouse apps still favor commercial platforms (Cin7, Unleashed, Fishbowl, SkuVault, Katana, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, self-hosting, and avoiding per-user or order-volume lock-in.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Track parts and stock in InvenTree or ERPNext → manage BOMs and manufacturing orders → connect sales channels via API or connectors → run purchasing and reordering rules → report with built-in or open BI tools. Suitable for manufacturers, distributors, and ecommerce sellers with technical capacity. Many growing product businesses still adopt commercial inventory platforms for faster multi-channel and 3PL integration.
