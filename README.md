## 📊 Market Overview & Industry Dynamics

> 💡 **Market Size & Structure**: The global inventory management software market is estimated at **$2.5 Billion+** (growing at ~10% CAGR). The market is **moderately fragmented**: enterprise ERP giants (like Zoho, Oracle NetSuite, and SAP) hold major overall enterprise share, while specialized e-commerce and manufacturing niches feature agile players (like Katana, Cin7, and Fishbowl) alongside vibrant open-source alternatives (like Odoo, ERPNext, and InvenTree).
